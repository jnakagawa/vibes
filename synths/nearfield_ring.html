NEARFIELD

the ring — a sound piece for a circle of phones

Phones stand still at marked positions around a circle. A chord of sine tones, tuned from its own stretched spectrum, rotates around the ring — handed from phone to phone — and each voice beats against itself as it passes between two speakers.

Walk with the rotation and the chord stands still around you; walk against it and it rushes past. The piece happens in the air between the phones.

The host opens the control page and shows its QR code; every phone scans it and picks its position (or scans a printed code at its spot). No app, no accounts.
